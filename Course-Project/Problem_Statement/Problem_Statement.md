# Research Grant Management System

The project specification is Chapter 13 of the course-supplied [Build Book](Build_Book.md), preserved in full alongside this summary.

Faculty miss relevant funding calls scattered across agency websites and mailing lists. Literature review and novelty framing take days, while proposal narratives, budgets and compliance documents are prepared separately. The Dean R&D office needs a consolidated view of proposal progress, deadlines and compliance.

Develop a coordinator-plus-workers system that supports funding-call matching, literature and novelty review, proposal drafting, itemized budget preparation, compliance checks and submission packaging. Validate outputs against the selected call and institutional rules. Named human reviewers authorize the exact package before release.

Stakeholders are Faculty, HoD, Dean R&D and Research Office. The primary output is an evidence-grounded grant proposal and compliance package with a review decision, version record and audit trail.
